# Code of Conduct

mbtop is a small open-source project, and everyone who participates in it is expected to help keep it welcoming and technically constructive.

Participants must:

- be respectful and assume good intent;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- welcome different backgrounds, experience levels, and terminals;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sexualized behavior in project spaces.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when conduct violates these expectations. Report concerns privately to a maintainer through the repository hosting platform rather than posting sensitive details publicly.
